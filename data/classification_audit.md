# Role classification audit

125 real postings: 18 assigned to a measured role, 107 unclassified.

**How to use this.** For each title write one letter in the VERDICT column:

- `O` — genuinely OUT of scope (sales, finance, marketing, HR, legal...)
- `M` — MISSED: it really is Data Analyst / Backend Developer / Business Analyst and we should have caught it
- `A` — AMBIGUOUS: a reasonable person could go either way

Then count: **recall = assigned / (assigned + M)** among genuinely in-scope roles.
Right now "18 of 125" measures neither precision nor recall.
